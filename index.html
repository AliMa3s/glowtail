Glowtail: Little Light Run

A little light. A big escape. A free game for Android by ArfzCorp.

The Blackouts are coming: a swarm of tiny shadows that wants to swallow every light in the city. Run, jump, slide and soar as Pip, a brave little glow. Dodge traffic, grab coins, unlock new friends and face giant bosses like the Lantern Eater in Neon City and Verdant Vale.
